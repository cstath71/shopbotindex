Welcome to ShopBot Index

Thanks for subscribing to ShopBot Index.

ShopBot Index is the weekly index of what online stores tell AI shopping agents. Every Monday it checks each tracked store for a UCP profile, the /.well-known/ucp file that is the official machine-readable profile AI shopping agents read. After each crawl you'll get one short, free email: which stores started or stopped publishing a UCP profile, and what else changed that week.

Until the next issue, here's where to look:

- The index: https://shopbotindex.com/
  What Shopify stores, big retailers and other stores tell AI shopping agents: who publishes a UCP profile, week by week.

- What changed: https://shopbotindex.com/changes.html
  Joined, left and other changes compared with the previous crawl.

- Methodology: https://shopbotindex.com/methodology.html
  Exactly what we check, how we check it, and what it does and doesn't mean.

Questions, or a store you'd like checked? Just reply to this email. It goes to hello@shopbotindex.com.
