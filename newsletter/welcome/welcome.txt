Welcome to ShopBot Index

Thanks for subscribing to ShopBot Index.

Every Monday the index checks each tracked store for a UCP business profile, the /.well-known/ucp file that is the official machine-readable profile AI shopping agents read. After each crawl you'll get one short, free email: which stores started publishing a UCP profile, which stopped, and what else changed that week.

Until the next issue, here's where to look:

- The index: https://shopbotindex.com/
  Which stores publish a UCP profile, across Shopify stores, big retailers and other stores.

- What changed: https://shopbotindex.com/changes.html
  Joined, left and other changes compared with the previous crawl.

- Methodology: https://shopbotindex.com/methodology.html
  Exactly how each store is checked and how the numbers are produced.

Questions, or a store you'd like checked? Just reply to this email. It goes to hello@shopbotindex.com.
