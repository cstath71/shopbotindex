Welcome to ShopBot Index

Thanks for subscribing to ShopBot Index.

Every Monday the index checks each tracked store for a UCP profile, the /.well-known/ucp file that is the official machine-readable profile AI shopping agents read, plus four supporting signals: agent tools, AI rules in robots.txt, AI guidance files and product data. After each crawl you'll get one short, free email: which stores started or stopped publishing a UCP profile, which changed what they tell AI agents, and what else moved that week.
